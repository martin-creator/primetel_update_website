Four Pathways

How You Can Join Our Mission

01

Philanthropic & Funding Partners

Fuel our mobile outreach missions, subsidize emergency medicine funds for low-income patients, and maintain our USSD telehealth lines. We provide transparent quarterly impact reporting and audited annual accounts.

  • Direct-to-field clinical allocation
  • Audited financial accountability
  • Naming/sponsorship options for mobile clinic days
Visit Donation Portal
02

Volunteer Clinicians & Specialists

We welcome Tanzanian and international medical doctors, clinical officers, enrolled nurses, and psychologists to join our mobile outreach teams or conduct specialized clinical workshops.

  • Primary medical consultations on field camps
  • Specialized mental health counseling
  • Community health worker training
Apply to Volunteer
03

Institutional & NGO Alliances

We partner with ministries, regional referral hospitals, universities, and international NGOs to run longitudinal research, public health campaigns, and health technology pilots.

  • Joint health interventions in Arusha region
  • Public health research & data collection
  • Corporate health & workplace CSR programs
Initiate Institutional Discussion
04

School & Youth Alliances

Secondary schools, colleges, and youth organizations partner with our psychosocial team to deliver structured mental wellness curriculums and peer-support mentor networks.

  • On-campus youth mental wellness workshops
  • Destigmatizing mental health & depression
  • Peer counselor mentorship training
Request School Partnership
