Common Questions

Operations & Visits FAQ

Where is the Primetel clinic physically located?

Our licensed outpatient healthcare clinic is in Monduli, Arusha region, Tanzania. We are open Monday through Saturday for consultations, diagnostic screenings, and maternal care.

How do I support patient care or send a donation?

You can visit our dedicated Support & Donation page to make a direct contribution via Tanzania Mobile Money (M-Pesa / Tigo Pesa) or bank transfer to fund patient visits and mobile clinic missions.

Can schools request a mental wellness workshop?

Yes. We conduct free mental wellness and resilience workshops for secondary schools across Arusha. Contact our schools team through the form above or email us directly at primetelhealth@gmail.com.
