Transparency & Responsibility

Where your contribution goes.

Primetel operates on an ultra-lean, grassroots model. Every shilling or dollar contributed directly supports clinical consumables, mobile outreach fuel, medication subsidies, and mental health clinicians serving in rural Monduli and Arusha.

01

Essential Medicine & Clinical Care

$25 / ~65,000 TZS

Covers comprehensive outpatient doctor consultation, malaria and blood sugar diagnostics, and essential prescriptions for a family unable to pay out-of-pocket fees at our Monduli clinic.

02

Youth Mental Wellness Workshop

$50 / ~130,000 TZS

Funds specialized counseling sessions and psychosocial training led by psychologist Albano Sabino for 50 secondary school students in Arusha, breaking stigma around depression and trauma.

03

Mobile Clinic Outreach to Remote Bomas

$100 / ~260,000 TZS

Sponsors vehicle transport, field diagnostic kits, and medical supplies for a mobile outreach team to visit a remote village in Monduli district where roads are impassable for public transport.

04

USSD Telehealth Bandwidth & Clinician Stipend

$250 / ~650,000 TZS

Maintains the low-tech mobile code line (*149*46#) accessible on non-smartphones, enabling 150+ rural patients to consult clinicians remotely.

Common Questions

Support & Accountability FAQ

Can I make a direct mobile money transfer from Tanzania?

Yes. Primetel accepts direct mobile money via Tanzania M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, and Airtel Money to our registered healthcare organizational accounts in Arusha. When you submit your pledge, you receive our official till details and a direct confirmation.

Can international donors wire funds directly?

Yes. We maintain institutional bank coordinates with SWIFT code access for direct wire transfers from partners and individual supporters in the US, Europe, and across Africa.

Can I sponsor a specific mobile outreach clinic or school program?

Absolutely. If your organization, church, family, or foundation wishes to sponsor an entire outreach mission or secondary school program in Monduli, please specify this in your pledge note or contact Founder Emmanuel Samwel directly.

How can I review past expenditures and clinical outcomes?

Primetel publishes comprehensive annual reports and clinical reviews on our Annual Reports page, documenting our patient numbers, school sessions, and financial accounts.
