Our Roots

How an idea in Monduli became a movement for healthcare equity.

Primetel was founded in Monduli, Arusha, by Emmanuel Christopher Samwel, an African Leadership Academy alumnus and health advocate who witnessed the systemic failures facing rural Tanzanians. In remote bomas and villages, routine infections became life-threatening simply because the nearest health center was hours away over impassable roads.

At the same time, mental healthcare was virtually absent from public conversations. Stigma kept individuals suffering in silence, and community health workers lacked the diagnostic tools and clinical backing to provide meaningful, long-term support.

What began as grassroots mental health and health literacy camps quickly evolved. Emmanuel brought together committed Tanzanian clinicians — led by Dr. Epiphania Simon Moshi and Dr. Gy Elly Martin — and counselling psychologist Albano Sabino to build a permanent continuum of care.

Today, Primetel is officially licensed as a medical clinic by the Ministry of Health. We combine brick-and-mortar primary care in Monduli with mobile field units, secondary school youth workshops, and a low-tech USSD telemedicine system that operates on simple feature phones without internet access.

"We didn't set out to build an abstract technology startup. We set out to treat patients who were being left behind by traditional infrastructure."

— Emmanuel C. Samwel, Founder & CEO

Licensed Healthcare Clinic

Operating under the standards and oversight of the Ministry of Health, United Republic of Tanzania. All clinical consultations and treatments are conducted by registered medical practitioners.

Our Mission

Universal access without geographic penalty.

To make quality, affordable, and culturally respectful healthcare accessible to every Tanzanian — regardless of geographic isolation, socioeconomic standing, or technological barrier. We bridge the last mile through integrated clinical practice, mobile outreach, and low-barrier digital tools.

Our Vision

A nation where health equity is lived reality.

A Tanzania where healthcare barriers are eliminated, where mental wellness is integrated seamlessly into primary care, and where no mother, child, or student suffers in silence due to lack of diagnostic screening or clinician support.

The Trajectory

Milestones in Our Journey

How Primetel grew from grassroots camps to an institutional healthcare provider.

2020

Foundation & Grassroots Health Camps

Emmanuel Samwel establishes Primetel in Monduli, identifying critical gaps in maternal healthcare and mental wellness across rural Arusha communities.

2022

USSD Telemedicine Launch

Primetel launches *149*46#, enabling any phone in Tanzania to connect patients with clinical triage and medical follow-up without internet access.

2024

Licensed Medical Clinic Status

Primetel receives formal clinical licensing from the Ministry of Health, establishing a permanent outpatient medical facility in Monduli.

2025–2026

Expansion: 45,000+ Reached

Mobile clinics scale across Arusha region; school mental health reaches 15,000+ students; strategic engagements with Ministry of Health leadership expand national advocacy.

Emmanuel Christopher Samwel

Founder & CEO

Emmanuel Samwel is a health advocate, digital innovator, and youth development champion dedicated to redefining healthcare access in Tanzania. As the Founder and CEO of Primetel Health, his work blends community health systems, technological delivery, and impact entrepreneurship. He has contributed to digital health initiatives with Doctors Without Borders and advised early-stage health ventures.

An African Leadership University graduate and African Leadership Academy alumnus, Emmanuel has earned recognition as a Millennium Fellow, an Innovate for Good Winner, and a Regional Finalist in the Hult Prize. Certified in mHealth, HRH systems, and mobile tech for health, he leads Primetel's mission to bridge rural healthcare divides.

Dr. Epiphania Simon Moshi

Medical in Charge (Doctor of Medicine)

Dr. Epiphania Simon Moshi is a dedicated Medical Doctor with comprehensive training and hands-on experience across hospital and clinical settings in Tanzania. She earned her Doctor of Medicine (MD) degree from Kilimanjaro Christian Medical University College (KCMUCo) in 2020.

Dr. Moshi completed her medical internship at Mt. Meru Regional Referral Hospital, managing complex inpatient and outpatient cases, conducting clinical assessments, and performing surgical and obstetric care — ranging from deliveries and cesarean sections to trauma stabilization and minor surgery. At Primetel, she leads the clinical staff and ensures all care adheres to national medical guidelines.

Dr. Gy Elly Martin

Medical Doctor

Gy Elly Martin is an experienced Clinical Officer with over five years of dedicated service in primary and specialized healthcare. A graduate of Machame Health Training Institution, he has built a strong clinical foundation combining technical expertise with patient-centered bedside care.

He has demonstrated deep proficiency in managing outpatient consultations, conducting diagnostic assessments, delivering emergency intervention, and supporting rural outreach programs in Monduli and Arusha.

Dr. Keneth H. Masao

Medical Clinician

Keneth H. Masao is a committed Enrolled Nurse with over five years of frontline healthcare experience across clinic and community settings. He specializes in clinical patient assessment, wound care, medication administration, and maternal health education.

Albano Sabino
