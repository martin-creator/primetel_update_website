How We Work

The Connected Healthcare Journey.

True community healthcare cannot stop at a single prescription. Primetel connects every stage of community health — from initial village listening to mobile outreach, clinic treatment, low-tech digital access, and longitudinal patient follow-up.

01 Community Grounding & Listening

Listening to pastoralist elders and remote bomas.

In Monduli district and surrounding rural Arusha, healthcare challenges begin with distance, cultural misunderstandings, and poverty. Primetel sits with community elders, local leaders, and families to design healthcare that respects cultural realities rather than imposing top-down assumptions.

02 Mobile Outreach & Field Care

Bringing diagnostics and treatment across the terrain.

When patients cannot afford fare or walk ten miles in ill health, our mobile medical units carry essential supplies, rapid diagnostic tests, and licensed clinicians directly to school grounds, churches, and village centers.

How Mobile Outreach Works
03 Licensed Clinical Care & Mental Health

Medical excellence combined with destigmatized therapy.

At our Monduli medical center, Dr. Epiphania Simon Moshi and Dr. Gy Elly Martin oversee primary healthcare, minor surgical procedures, antenatal checks, and chronic illness management. Alongside medical care, psychologist Albano Sabino leads confidential counseling to break the silence surrounding mental health.

04 & 05 Digital Health & Patient Follow-Up

Connecting basic phones to continuous care.

Rather than building an app that requires smartphones and data bundles that our patients cannot afford, Primetel built on USSD. A mother with a 15-year-old feature phone can dial *149*46# to register symptoms, request medicine refills, or receive clinician callbacks.

Community health champions then follow up in person, verifying medication adherence and monitoring recovery over months — not just days.
